It originally comes from the DMZ, but young job seekers use it to mean the psychological line they refuse to go below. For engineers, that line is Pyeongtaek, so if a company is located any further south, they simply don't apply, no matter how good the offer is. And the small things count too — a nice cafeteria with free meals, a shuttle bus running from Seoul, and a generous allowance for team dinners.

So all in all, young people today are looking for much more than just a high salary. That's it, Eva!
