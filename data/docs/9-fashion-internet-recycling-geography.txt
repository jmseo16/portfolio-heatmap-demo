9-8. <Please describe the geographic features of your country>


Okay, I would like to describe Korea peninsula as "a land of high mountains and numerous islands". Even though the land is small, there are many attractive geographic features to check out.

First off, there's the Taebaek mountain range, the longest and highest range in Korea, running through Gangwon-do. Gangwon-do is famous for its heavy snow thanks to this range, and along its slopes, you'll find plenty of resorts where people enjoy skiing, sledding, you name it, so tons of people flock there every winter to make the most of it. And it's not just winter, the range is also dotted with valleys and rivers, so once summer comes around, people head there to cool down from the scorching heat as well.

Then there's Jeju, Korea's largest island, which was actually formed by volcanic eruptions long ago. When it comes to white sandy beaches and clear blue water, Jeju comes first. It's so called the "Hawaii of Korea" because of its emerald, crystal-clear water with palm trees lining the beach. And Jungmun Beach is the island's most iconic spot. Tons of people go there to hit the bars along the coast at night, and during the day, they go snorkeling or go surfing to appreciate the beautiful marine life.

So yeah, from snow-capped mountains to island beaches, those are all packed into a small peninsula. Eva, how's about your country?
